# Production Reference — I Did Not Come Here To Die

---

## Core Production Philosophy

Industrial techno and big beat frameworks carrying folk, labor, protest, murder ballad, and Irish rebel songs. The genre framework is there — the sonics, the weight, the aggression — but the rhythmic foundation is doing something the genre doesn't usually do.

Nothing accidental. Nothing haphazard. Nothing forgotten.

---

## Tempo

Binary. No middle ground.

- **Slow:** ~80bpm — weight, inevitability, dread, certainty
- **Fast:** ~180bpm — violence, momentum, urgency

Working tempo for loop construction: 95-105bpm (neutral, relationships audible, easier to add/divide/mix). Push or pull to final tempo when the architecture is solid.

First instinct on direction is always correct.

---

## Rhythm Approach

- Snare as primary timing reference (not kick)
- Each drum element tracked separately, snare first
- No click track — feel and count only
- Fractional polyrhythmic relationships: 7/9, 13/20, 14/19, prime number relationships
- Euclidean trigger patterns from modular for basslines
- Snare placement on 1, 5, 9, 13 of 16-step explored for slower tracks

**Current loop in development:**
- 4/16 kick foundation
- 909 snare on 1, 5, 9, 13
- 13/20 hats (playing live)
- Working at 82bpm — wants to go slower
- CR-78 potentially joining

## Drum Machines / Electronic Percussion

- **CR-78:** Primary atmospheric/texture drum — organic, slightly apologetic, suits slower material. Visual: fuzzy spikey low colors with angles twisting inward. Calibration reference: In The Air Tonight loop, 96bpm.
- **808:** Clean, geometric, forward momentum
- **909:** Snap and attack, dance music backbone

## Key Signal Chains

### James Connolly Heartbeat
Stroll (TV Yellow Les Paul Special, P-90s) B string  
→ Coppersound Triplegraph (octave down mode)  
→ Moog 24dB filter  

*The Triplegraph octave down preserves guitar harmonic character at bass register — sounds like something that shouldn't exist at that frequency. Moog filter controls how much P90 character and upper harmonic content survives.*

### Industrial Clang Element
Dead blow hammer wrapped in copper → personal anvil  
*Copper wrap softens initial transient, extends decay, removes pure metallic ping. Anvil's mass and history in the resonance. At 80bpm the decay has room to be present before absorption.*

## Microphone Placement

**Gretsch 14" brass snare (vintage, slightly abused):**  
SM57, high off-center, 38.25 degrees  

*Placement optimized for this specific drum. The abuse and the brass shell require a mic that tells the truth — the 57 does.*

## Instruments

### Primary
- **Stroll** — TV Yellow Les Paul Special, P-90s, heavily checked, dings, sounds better every time. Angry joyful locked chaos. No purity, no consistency.
- **Gretsch snare** — 14", brass, vintage, slightly abused. The soul of this record.
- **Magnatone Baby M-80** — Chrome Grape Billy Gibbons signature. Favorite amp. Pitch-based tremolo circuit.
- **Personal anvil** — older than the studio, NASA-era provenance. Labor tradition made literal.

### Modular
125 modules. Transformation engine as much as sound source.  
Key roles on this record:
- Rhythmic displacement (Euclidean patterns, LFO modulation)
- Processing of atmospheric recordings (partner's voice, environmental sounds)
- Texture and atmosphere in spaces the drums and guitar don't occupy
- Long-period cascading LFO for emergent results over time

---

## Atmospheric Recordings

**Partner's voice:**  
Recordings of speaking, quiet singing, moving, ambient sounds — gathered without disclosure. To be heavily processed through modular. Present as haunting, not as feature. Do not announce. Do not reveal capture until catalog is sufficient and unguarded quality is protected.

**Children:**  
Natural recordings gathered first. House of the Rising Sun chorus potential — summer project. Reference: Lillie Marsh on Dear God (XTC). The innocence that doesn't belong there making everything more true.

## Scale Reference

**James Connolly:** Hungarian Minor  
- F# or A# — determine against drum loop, by feel  
- Augmented second between 6th and 7th degrees creates unresolved ancient tension  
- Outside Western folk tradition — nobody has walked this territory with this song
