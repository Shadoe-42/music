# Ella Speed — Arrangement Notes

**Position:** Side One, Track 3  
**Tempo:** Propulsive, forward momentum  
**PD Status:** Traditional / Lead Belly — clear  

---

## Why This Song

A murder ballad with emotional flatness. Bill Martin shoots Ella Speed and the song keeps moving. The matter-of-fact coldness is more disturbing than grief would be. That detachment is already industrial in its psychology.

The cold narrator voice suits a deep register better than a wiry folk voice. Not lamenting. Reporting.

---

## Arrangement Architecture

### The Crash

The defining production decision on this track.

- A single crash sits above everything at the moment of the shooting
- NOT isolated — not the only thing you feel, but the thing you feel MOST in that moment
- Everything else ducked to some degree, but not to silence
- The ducking trails as the crash decays — the world reasserting itself around the event
- Everything was always still there. It just got quiet for a moment out of something that isn't quite respect.
- Then: continues on like nothing. The groove doesn't stop. Bill Martin is already walking away.

**The subtle duck is correct — not silence.** A flinch, not a stop. The machinery barely noticed.

**Crash characteristics:**
- Needs to sit in a frequency space already occupied, not cleared for it
- Emerges from the texture rather than interrupting it
- Decay length = emotional duration of the moment (shorter = life resumes fast)
- Something with decay that gets swallowed fast — present then immediately absorbed back into texture

### Rhythm
- 7/9 hat relationship under consideration (originally built for this track or Dig a Hole)
- Propulsive, not languorous — the world continuing its relentless pace

---

## Thematic Notes

The indifference is the whole song.

The production choice of continuing without dramatic pause after the crash is not a statement about violence — it's the truth of how the song already works. Guthrie and Lead Belly understood that some things just happen and the world moves on. The arrangement honors that.

The crash that doesn't stop everything is more disturbing than the one that does.
